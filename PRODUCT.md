# Product: Perfect X Contracting website

## Platform
web · static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (index.html plus an img/ folder), hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- People in Vermont ("Serving Vermont", the business's own words; no towns published) who need drywall installed, taped and finished, or repaired: homeowners, and builders or general contractors (inferred from where the work was posted: a contractor referral group, and the job itself, a commercial four-story stairwell).
- They arrive from Facebook on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner (Facebook page "Perfect x contracting") is the first audience: the site is handed to them free. Owner name unknown; copy avoids it.

## Purpose
1. Show the owner their business done better than they imagined, within one phone screen.
2. Turn a visitor into a free-estimate request through the only channel the business publishes: Facebook Messenger.

## Positioning
A drywall crew (inferred "we": the caption says "Message us") whose pitch is finish quality: "completed with precision and care", "Quality workmanship and attention to detail from start to finish", "Quality work. Reliable service."

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: Facebook Messenger only (`https://m.me/61595304966148`, checked 2026-10-19: redirects to a Messenger thread). No phone, no email, no address published.
- The estimate form composes a message the visitor copies and pastes into Messenger (m.me links can't pre-fill text). Copy may be blocked in the in-app browser, so the message stays visible and selectable.
- A `PHONE` constant in the page switches the form to a text-message handoff and shows the number top-right once a number is confirmed.
- No prices, reviews, licensing, insurance, hours or years in business: none stated, none claimed.

## Brand commitments
- Logo (Facebook profile image, 720px): brushed-silver "PX" monogram with a violet X under a violet roofline with a four-pane window; "PERFECT X" in heavy silver caps, "CONTRACTING" spaced below between violet dashes; on black.
- Colors from the logo: violet (~#7B2CF5 to #A15BFF), brushed silver. The page uses a deep violet-slate ground instead of the logo's black.
- Name: the Facebook page renders "Perfect x contracting"; the logo sets "PERFECT X CONTRACTING". The page uses "Perfect X Contracting" in running text (inferred from the logo) — confirm with the owner.
- Voice: proud craftsman, showcase-style, "we".

## Evidence on hand
- Logo (Facebook og:image).
- One 77-second phone video (720x1280) from the Facebook group post: a four-story stairwell, boards hung, taped and spotted, capped windows. No people in the frames used.
- Caption, verbatim: "PROJECT SHOWCASE — PERFECT X CONTRACTING. Four-story staircase drywall project with capped windows, completed with precision and care! Quality workmanship and attention to detail from start to finish. ... Quality work. Reliable service." and "Need drywall installation, taping and finishing, or repairs? Message us today for a free estimate!"

- Only facts from the brief and the owner's own posts.
